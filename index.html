copy board · what sells to small business owners

Why they'd buy

A bakery owner doesn't care about Python. She cares that the last web guy vanished, the site is broken on phones, and she feels dumb asking about it. "You Come First" answers each of those. Every message below names a fear and then makes a promise.

Fear: getting ghosted

She texts back

"Finally, a tech person who calls you back."

The number one complaint about freelancers is that they disappear. Our version of an AI girlfriend is one who actually answers.

Fear: feeling dumb

No jargon

"She'll explain it like a person, not a manual."

Owners stall because tech talk makes them feel small. Promise plain English and no eye-rolling.

Fear: agency prices

Small and mighty

"Your little app, without the big agency."

A small matched team, sized to a small business. One brief in, one team out, which is the site's own promise.

Fear: waiting forever

Fast, and good

"AI makes her fast. Experience makes her good."

This borrows the site's best line: AI made building faster, but it did not make everyone good at building.

Fear: a site that doesn't sell

Built to sell

"A booking button beats a pretty homepage."

Talk results, not tech: works on phones, takes bookings, takes orders, shows up on Google.

Fear: someone else's mess

Rescue me

"Your nephew built it with AI. She'll fix it."

Vibe Code Rescue is a real offer on the site. Lots of owners now have a half-working AI-built site, so this one sells itself.
