# AI Tech Girlfriends: Style Guide

**v0.1 · concept · October 2026**

AI Tech Girlfriends (aitechgirlfriends.com) is a collective of women who build with AI. We make websites and little apps for small business owners. This guide covers the look, the type, the voice and the merch, so anyone making something for the brand makes it the same way.

---

## 1. The big idea

**The girlfriends are real.**

An "AI girlfriend" is usually a fake person in an app. Ours are real women who build with AI. The brand borrows the AI-girlfriend setup screen (pick her hair, set her personality) and then reveals that every option is a real skill.

**Tagline:** You Come First.
It works three ways: the client comes first, she texts back, and yes, the wink.

**The one rule under everything:** show a real hand made it. Cut paper, tape, wobble and ink smudge are proof, not mistakes.

---

## 2. Who we're talking to

Small business owners who need a website or a little app: a bakery, salon, boutique, studio, food truck or practice.

They don't care about code. They care about four fears, and every piece of copy answers one of them:

| Their fear | Our promise | Line to use |
|---|---|---|
| Getting ghosted by a freelancer | She texts back | "Finally, a tech person who calls you back." |
| Feeling dumb about tech | No jargon | "She'll explain it like a person, not a manual." |
| Agency prices | Small and mighty | "Your little app, without the big agency." |
| Waiting forever | Fast, and good | "AI makes her fast. Experience makes her good." |
| A site that doesn't sell | Built to sell | "A booking button beats a pretty homepage." |
| Someone else's AI-built mess | Rescue me | "Your nephew built it with AI. She'll fix it." |

## 3. Voice

- **Plain words.** 8th-grade reading level. Short sentences. Active verbs.
- **Sweet on top, cheeky underneath.** Never crude. The wink does the work.
- **Talk results, not tech.** Say "works on phones" and "takes bookings," not "responsive" or "API."
- **Name their business back to them.** "Your salon's site is live" beats "Your site is live."
- **No apologies, no corporate filler.**

**Promise carefully.** Don't claim a price, a timeline or a response time until the collective agrees it can deliver. The two strongest claims to add once they're true: "price agreed up front" and "live in weeks."

### Ad lines

- Your website's new girlfriend. She texts back.
- No jargon. No ghosting. You come first.
- For the bakery that's been "fixing the website" since 2019. *(swap the business)*
- She'll build your booking app. You'll actually understand it.
- Built fast with AI. Built right by women who know what they're doing.
- Each girlfriend comes with: a site that works on phones, a booking button, and a person who answers.
- Tell her what your business needs. She'll make it exist.
- Warning: may cause new customers.

## 4. Colour

Toy-aisle pink and zine paper. Hot pink is the hero, so use it with purpose. Paper, cream and ink do most of the work.

| Name | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hot pink | `#FF2E88` | The hero. Box, buttons, the one thing that matters. |
| Deep pink | `#C4005C` | Shadows under the logo, pressed states, headline text on cream. |
| Aisle pink | `#FDE3EC` | Page background, with a faint pink dot grid. |
| Cream | `#FFF8EF` | Cards, the box nameplate, paper. |
| Ink | `#2A1420` | Body text and fine print. A warm near-black, never pure black. |
| Muted | `#7D5A68` | Labels and secondary text. |
| Mint | `#A8ECD2` | Cut-out paper and accents. Pairs with deep pink. |

| Tape | `#FF78AA` at 55% | Washi tape on photos and cards. |

## 5. Type

Four fonts, each with one job. All are free Google Fonts, so they're safe to print and sell.

| Job | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut-out letters** | Archivo Black (base), Abril Fatface, Bungee, Rubik Mono One, Silkscreen, Special Elite | Built letter by letter, never just typed. See section 6. |
| **Doll-box logo** | Shrikhand | Retro 80s toy-box script with a stacked pink shadow. *Option:* Bagel Fat One if we lean more Y2K. |
| **Handwritten notes** | Caveat | Captions only, never a paragraph. *Option:* Rock Salt for a messier, more punk feel. |
| **Reading text** | Karla | Body copy. Stays quiet so the loud fonts can be loud. |
| **Labels and fine print** | Courier Prime | Uppercase, letter-spaced. Box fine print, tags, prices. |

**Don't use** Inter, Helvetica or Poppins for big headlines; they read as generic.
**If there's ever a budget:** Editorial New or Canela in place of Abril, and Eurostile for gadget-box labels. Check licences first.

## 6. Cut-out letters (the signature)

The "You Come First" lettering looks like a ransom note made from tech magazines.

- **One letter per scrap of paper.** Each scrap gets a different font and paper colour.
- **Mix upper and lower case** at random.
- **Tilt each letter** between −4° and +4°, and nudge it up or down a couple of pixels.
- **Give each scrap a tiny hard shadow** (1px across, 2px down) so it looks glued on.
- **Keep words together.** A line can break between words, never inside one.
- **Same words, same look.** The random pattern is seeded by the text, so "You Come First" always comes out the same way.
- **For print, make it by hand.** Cut real letters from thrifted 90s computer magazines, glue them onto cream paper with pink washi tape, and scan at 600 dpi.

## 7. The doll box

The brand's hero object is a vintage fashion-doll box. It's also the shirt graphic.

**Anatomy, top to bottom:**

1. **Logo:** "AI Tech Girlfriends" in Shrikhand, white, tilted −3°, stacked deep-pink shadow.
2. **Tagline:** "You Come First" in cut-out letters.
3. **Window:** an arched cellophane window with a soft pink glow, little stars and a diagonal shine. The doll stands inside.
4. **Starburst:** gold, tilted, naming her top trait ("NO jargon!", "TEXTS back!").
5. **Close-up circle:** her face, so you can see her eye colour.
6. **Side tray:** "comes with!" and two accessories, each labelled as a real perk.
7. **Nameplate:** cream panel with her name, her type and a "with…" line.
8. **Fine print stripe:** ink background. "Real women · No jargon · ⚠ Warning: may cause new customers · Small business approved."

**Her types** come from her strongest trait:

| Trait | Type |
|---|---|
| Plain English | The Plain-English Princess |
| Texts back | The Always-Texts-Back Angel |
| Speed | The Ship-It Sweetheart |
| Makes it pretty | The Website Bestie |
| Tech brains | The Little App Baddie |

**Accessories are perks:** pink laptop = your new website, coffee = weekly check-ins, debug duck = bug fixes, headphones = she listens.

**Names** come from women in computing: Ada, Grace, Hedy, Radia, Katherine, Margaret, Annie.

### The doll herself

- **She must be original.** Not Barbie, not Bratz, no real toy brand. Borrow the era, never the character.
- **Today's dolls are AI stand-ins**, and they still look too close to a famous doll. Before anything is sold, reshoot with a real doll or commission an original one.
- **Range matters.** Every hair style comes in light, tan and deep skin, with brown, hazel, blue or green eyes.
- **Outfit:** hot pink satin bomber, white tee with a tiny red heart, light-wash jeans, white sneakers.

## 8. Motion

The motion is the hand at work. The camera stays still, and every loop is seamless.

- **Boil:** cut-out letters flip between three hand-placed poses at 8 frames a second, like stop-motion.
- **Swap:** when you change her look, the doll fades down and back up (about 0.25s, ease-out).
- **Talk:** press her heart and a comic speech bubble pops in with a slight overshoot, then fades after about 3 seconds.
- **Stamp:** for candy-heart pieces, the words press in big and blurry, then settle.
- **Keep it calm:** 2–3 moving things on screen at most. Respect "reduce motion" settings.
- **Video:** every mp4 loops seamlessly with a locked camera. No zooms or push-ins.

## 9. Merch

- **Launch shirt:** the cut-out "You Come First" lettering, made by hand and scanned. Print DTG to keep the paper texture, or reduce it to 3 spot colours for screen print. Cream or black tee.
- **Second shirt:** the doll box as a full front graphic.
- **Back print or sticker:** "Your website's new girlfriend. She texts back."
- **Event giveaway:** real letter-bead friendship bracelets ("YOU ♡ COME ♡ FIRST", "SHIP IT", "AI GF") to trade at events.
- **Later drops:** a candy-heart series with one line per shirt (YOU COME FIRST, SHIP IT, TEXT ME BACK, PUSH TO PROD).

---

## 10. Do and don't

**Do**
- Let it look handmade and a little wonky.
- Keep hot pink for the moments that matter.
- Say what she does for their business, in their words.
- Mix sweet and punk in the same piece.

**Don't**
- Make it look like a clean SaaS template.
- Use a real toy brand's doll, logo, box or name.
- Promise prices, timelines or response times we haven't agreed on.
- Get crude. The wink is the whole joke.
